## Background & Motivation

Evidence-based transport planning is essential for reducing carbon emissions, improving safety, and enabling sustainable mobility at scale.

Understanding electric vehicles, charging demand, parking patterns is critical for advancing safe and sustainable transport systems. Despite prior work, significant gaps remain — particularly in how we translate research findings into scalable, real-world tools.
