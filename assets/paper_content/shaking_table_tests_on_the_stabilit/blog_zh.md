## 研究概述

Benched rock slopes are prevalent in extensive engineering endeavors such as mining and road construction. This research investigates the dynamic response patterns and failure mechanisms of dip and anti-dip rock slopes through shaking table tests. The adopted approach involves utilizing the acceleration amplification factor (AAF) to compare the amplification effects on both slope types under varying excitation amplitudes. Fourier spectrum analysis of acceleration is conducted to explore the relationship between seismic wave frequency and slope response. The Hilbert-Huang Transform (HHT) method is employed to analyze the differences in seismic response between dip and anti-dip slopes.
