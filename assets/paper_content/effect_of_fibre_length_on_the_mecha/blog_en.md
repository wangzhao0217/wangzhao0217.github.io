## Overview

Despite significant amount of research on Recycled Tyre Steel Fibres (RTSF – extracted from end-of-life tyres) as reinforcement in concrete slab applications, the potential of RTSF is not fully understood mainly due to their variability in length (and aspect ratio). This paper investigates the effect of fibre length on the mechanical properties of Steel Fibre Reinforced Concrete (SFRC) using Recycled Tyre Cord Filaments (RTCF - extracted from unvulcanised rubber tyre belt off-cuts) which have very similar mechanical properties to RTSF. The flexural behaviour of SFRC prisms is evaluated using fixed lengths of RTCF (6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21 and 24 mm) at a dosage of 30 kg/m3.
