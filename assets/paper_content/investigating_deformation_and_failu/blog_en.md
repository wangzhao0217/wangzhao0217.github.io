# Investigating deformation and failure mechanisms of discontinuous anti-dip bench rock slopes through shaking table tests and numerical simulations

## Overview

Large-scale shaking table tests and numerical simulations were conducted to investigate the deformation and failure mecha- nisms of an anti-dip bench rock slope with discontinuities. The study introduces the Displacement Baseline Offset Ratio (DBOR) to characterize the instability and failure processes of slopes when they reach a plastic state under seismic excita- tions. It examines the peak ground displacement and earth pressure responses, as well as the cumulative damage processes of the slope model to provide insight into the deformation and failure mechanisms. The results show a significant displacement amplification effect at elevated points of the slope when subjected to seismic excitations.
